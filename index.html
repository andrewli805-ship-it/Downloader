Powering Our City

An interactive guide to Energy for Hong Kong Secondary students. Aligned with HKDSE curriculum standards for Physics and Integrated Science.

Forms of Energy Conservation Law Renewables

Fundamental Concepts

Kinetic Energy (KE)

Energy possessed by an object due to its motion. In HKDSE, calculated as:

KE = ½mv²

Gravitational PE (PE)

Energy stored in an object due to its position in a gravitational field.

PE = mgh

Conservation Law

Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it can only be converted from one form to another.

Total Energy = Constant

Hong Kong's Energy Mix

How does a world-class city like Hong Kong stay powered 24/7? Our electricity comes from two major power companies: CLP (New Territories, Kowloon, Lantau) and HK Electric (HK Island).

EMSD | Mandatory Efficiency Scheme (MEELS)

Mandatory Energy Efficiency Labelling Scheme (MEELS)

In Hong Kong, electrical appliances like air conditioners, refrigerators, and washing machines must display an energy label. Grade 1 (Green) is the most energy-efficient, while Grade 5 (Red) is the least.

  • ✔ Saves money on electricity bills
  • ✔ Reduces HK's overall carbon footprint
  • ✔ Informs consumers before purchase
  • ✔ Regulated by EMSD (Gov HK)
